About me

Mario is a UI/UX Designer and Frontend Developer who believes that great design isn't just about how it looks, but how it works. My journey began with a fascination for digital art and how they work, and it has evolved into a career dedicated to turning complex user problems into intuitive, high-impact digital solutions.

I design with style and equally build the function logic through codes that make them work. Whether I’m wireframing in Figma or writing clean, responsive code in CSS and Javascript, my goal is always the same: to create products that feel as good as they look.

Based in Abuja, I thrive in environments that challenge my creative perspective and technical skills. When I’m not pushing pixels, you’ll find me in a sports stadium or in the gym. I am currently looking to bring my blend of design and development to a forward-thinking team at a creative agency.
